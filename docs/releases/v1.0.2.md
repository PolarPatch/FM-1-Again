# FM-1 Again 1.0.2

- **The groove's notes out (NOTES OUT, on by default, GLO › MIDI):** what the groove plays goes out as MIDI notes over
  USB and Bluetooth: the drums on channel 10 (General MIDI drum notes), the chords on channel 4, RISE on channel 5; the
  keys stay on channel 1. STOP sends all notes off on those channels. A companion app (FM-1 Live) lights its keys and
  names the chords from them.
- **Screen mirroring draws the whole screen at once** when a viewer asks for it (APP_REDRAW), the boot screen and the
  screensaver too.
- **No flicker with mirroring on:** the whole-screen redraws draw over the screen, never clear it first, and come at
  most every 2 s, so a viewer never falls behind while the groove plays. Waking from the screensaver mends it at once.
- **HOME's header** shows the groove or the bar, then the chord playing now (Am9, Fmaj9), instead of the name and tempo.
- **MIDI in:** pitch bend (two semitones) and the mod wheel (a vibrato) on KEYS, VOX and CHOP; a sustain pedal (CC 64);
  CC 7 as the volume; CC 120 / 123 let every note go.
- **CHANNEL (GLO › MIDI):** OMNI (the default) or one channel 1-16, for MIDI in and the keys out.
- **The screen reader** says HOME on HOME, PLAYING after the knobs, and FILTER as one word (LP50, HP33).
- **KEYS is lighter on the CPU** (the tremolo once a frame for all voices); the sound is the same.
