# FM-1 Again 1.0.2

- **The groove's notes out (NOTES OUT, on by default, GLO › MIDI):** what the groove plays goes out as MIDI notes over
  USB and Bluetooth: the drums on channel 10 (General MIDI drum notes), the chords on channel 4, RISE on channel 5; the
  keys stay on channel 1. STOP sends all notes off on those channels. A companion app (FM-1 Live) lights its keys and
  names the chords from them.
- **Screen mirroring draws the whole screen at once** when a viewer asks for it (APP_REDRAW), the boot screen and the
  screensaver too.
