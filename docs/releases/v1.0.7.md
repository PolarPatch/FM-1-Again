# FM-1 Again 1.0.7

- **MIDI over Wi-Fi:** with **NETMIDI** on (the LINK page, K4) and the FM-1 and a phone on the same network
  (WI-FI AP is simplest), the FM-1 finds the phone's network MIDI session by itself and joins it. The page then
  says MIDI. What you play reaches apps such as Ableton Note ("Network Session 1" under its MIDI input), beside
  Bluetooth MIDI or in its place. A computer can add the FM-1 to its network session too (port 5004), and notes
  that come in from the session play FM-1 Again.
- **The manual** shows how to send the notes to a phone over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, with Ableton Note's steps.
